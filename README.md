# Fuli Yang
### Financial econometrics · institutional market dynamics · distributional risk

[Academic homepage](https://fuliyang.io/) · [GARCH Institute](https://garch.institute/) · [All papers on SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/cf_dev/AbsByAuth.cfm?per_id=11814228)

I study how institutions, information and market design shape financial risk, and develop methods for measuring volatility, distributional change and market memory.

## Research programmes

- **[Institutional Market Dynamics (IMD)](https://imd.garch.institute/)** — shared constraints, institutional synchronization and market covariance.
- **[Distributional Regime Persistence (DRP)](https://drp.garch.institute/)** — distributional geometry, market memory and stress measurement.
