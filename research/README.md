# Research guide

[Research hub](../README.md) · [Eight selected papers](../README.md#selected-papers) · [All papers on SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/cf_dev/AbsByAuth.cfm?per_id=11814228)

The hub presents eight representative papers. The full bibliography is available on SSRN.

## Institutional Market Dynamics

IMD studies how shared institutional constraints synchronize portfolio demand and reshape market covariance. It connects institutional theory, constraint geometry and empirical market design.

[IMD programme website](https://imd.garch.institute/)

## Distributional Regime Persistence

DRP develops geometric measures of financial return distributions, market memory and stress. Its strands cover real-time monitoring, conditional tail information, portability and representation complexity.

[DRP programme website](https://drp.garch.institute/)

## Volatility and crypto markets

GARCH-family models, apparent roughness, perpetual-futures funding, realized higher moments and tail risk.

## Japan macro-finance

JGB yield-curve shocks, yen intervention, carry unwinds, repatriation and cross-border portfolio flows.

## Energy and US macro-finance

Oil-price innovations, Treasury term premia and the interaction of energy infrastructure with financial risk.

## Monetary policy communication

FOMC language, voting rights, policy projections and their volatility content.

## Climate risk and AI

Climate signals, credit intermediation, disclosure measurement, AI investment, financing and market structure.

## Market design and institutions

IPOs, cornerstone participation, lockups, regulatory reform, index inclusion and compute derivatives.
